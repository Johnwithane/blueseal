## ✍️ DRAFT — Round 1 answers (≤100 words each, in the founders' voice)

> Edit freely; these are grounded in the live product. Word counts are within the 100-word cap.

**1. What is your product/service?**

> Blue Seal is a progressive web app that fixes the trust gap in hiring skilled tradespeople. We hand-verify every tradesperson's trade certification and government ID before their profile goes live, then run the entire job in one place: clients search verified pros or post a job and compare itemized quotes; the work is managed on a per-job board with chat, photos, and scheduling; and it closes with an auto-generated invoice, in-app card payment, and a mutual review where both sides rate each other. It's live in BC's Okanagan with roughly 100 verified tradespeople across 134 trades.

**2. What is innovative about it?**

> Three things. First, verification is done by a person — real credential and ID checks bind a verified profile to live marketplace state, not a self-claimed badge. Second, AI is built into each job: it reads the intake, chat, and photos to draft replies, generate itemized quotes, write invoice notes from tracked time and expenses, and scan receipts — specific to the job, not generic. Third, our capped service fee keeps high-ticket jobs on-platform where commission-based competitors leak. It's built on Vue 3, Firebase, and strict TypeScript, with default-deny security and validated function boundaries throughout.

**3. What is your business model?**

> Two revenue lines. A 5% service fee on card payments, paid by the client and capped at $99 — so tradespeople keep 100% of their invoice and big jobs stay on-platform. And Blue Seal Pro at $29/month, which unlocks the AI tools, waives the client fee, and adds a client CRM with recurring billing — turning lumpy transaction fees into predictable recurring revenue. Two more lines build on the same verified audience: insurance placement (in build now) and per-seat enterprise accounts. We build verified supply first, then pour in local demand — prove one region, then replicate the playbook.
