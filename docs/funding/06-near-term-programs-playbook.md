## A. Loans you can pursue now

### Community Futures Central Okanagan (CFDCCO) — up to $150,000
The local Community Futures serving Kelowna (Oyama → Peachland).

- **Amount:** up to **$150,000**, term up to 5 years.
- **Pre-revenue?** Yes — explicitly serves people "looking to **start**" a business.
- **Eligibility:** must be a **resident of the Central Okanagan** and operate there for the loan term (Kelowna qualifies). A realistic business plan required. Quirk: applicants "must **not be in a position to access traditional financing**" — a pre-revenue startup typically satisfies this.
- **Rate:** bank prime + 4–6%, plus a 2% processing fee (min $350).
- **Incorporation:** not stated as required — sole props not ruled out; confirm by phone.
- **Bonus:** business advisors + a Self-Employment / Business Plan Development Program.
- **Funds available:** Youth Loan Fund (18–29), a Community Business Loan Fund (incl. innovative technology / export), and a general fund (no stated age cap).
- ▶️ **Action:** Call **250-868-2132** (ext. 227/239) to scope a startup loan; reuse the narrative bank + cash flow. Good **complement or alternative** to Futurpreneur (and the fallback if the Futurpreneur age gate fails).
- *Source: [cfdcco.com/services/business-lending](https://cfdcco.com/services/business-lending).*

> *BDC Start-up Financing (up to $150K) needs **12 months operating + revenue** — not now; revisit ~1 year post-launch. Futurpreneur already bundles the BDC co-loan, which is how a pre-revenue founder reaches BDC money today (see `05`).*

## B. NRC IRAP — start the (free) relationship after incorporating

IRAP is **relationship-driven, not a written application.** You phone in, an advisor assesses fit, and funding (if any) is *invited* later.

- **Eligibility (hard gates):** **incorporated**, for-profit, operating in Canada, **≤500 FTE**, commercializing innovative tech. **No revenue threshold.** Sole props/partnerships are explicitly excluded → incorporate first.
- **Two things IRAP offers:** (1) **free advisory** from an Industrial Technology Advisor (ITA); (2) **non-repayable financial contributions** toward R&D salaries/contractors (cost-share up to 75% salaries / 50% contractors).
- **Honest timing:** funding is a **later thing** — the reimbursement model and the "management & financial capacity" assessment go against a day-1 startup. But the **advisor relationship is worth starting early** (it takes months to build) and is independent of any funding decision.
- **IRAP Youth Employment Program:** subsidy to hire a **15–30 post-secondary grad** into an R&D role (6–12 months, ≥30 hrs/wk). Covers "a portion" of salary *(the widely-cited "$30K" is **not** officially confirmed)*. You don't need to be an existing IRAP client. Needs incorporation + the hire + cash to front salary.
- ▶️ **Action (after incorporating):** a founder calls **1-877-994-4727**; frame it as **advice/fit, not a funding ask**. Build the relationship while you build the product. Don't use the generic NRC "Contact us" form — the phone line is the documented door.
- *Source: [nrc.canada.ca financial support for technology innovation](https://nrc.canada.ca/en/support-technology-innovation/financial-support-technology-innovation).*

## D. Deferred grants — milestones, not current applications

> Don't apply early — you'll be rejected on eligibility and burn goodwill. These become live as you hit
> traction/revenue/employee milestones. Keep them on the radar.

### Innovate BC Go-To-Market Microgrant — up to $50,000 (the one to aim for)
- **Amount:** up to **$50,000** non-repayable, covering **up to 75% of project costs** (you provide a **25% cash match**), $10K floor.
- **Why deferred:** requires being **incorporated + headquartered in BC + product already in-market with established product-market fit + secured funding/early revenue.** You meet none of the traction/revenue tests *yet*.
- ▶️ **Revisit when:** you have paid product-market fit (real paid jobs, early revenue) post-incorporation. This is your **#1 target grant** once traction exists — prep the project narrative from the narrative bank then.
- *Source: [innovatebc.ca/go-to-market-microgrant](https://www.innovatebc.ca/go-to-market-microgrant).*

### CanExport SMEs — up to $50,000 for export market development
- **Why deferred:** 2026-27 minimum is **3 full-time employees + $300K–$100M annual revenue.** You're far from both.
- ▶️ **Revisit when:** you have employees + revenue and are expanding into the US (Alberta is domestic; this is for *international* markets). Intake runs ~Feb–Aug each year.
- *Source: [tradecommissioner.gc.ca CanExport SMEs](https://www.tradecommissioner.gc.ca/en/our-solutions/funding-financing-international-business/canexport-smes/find-out-qualify.html).*
