# Incorporation Decision Memo — the key that unlocks the funding stack

> **Bottom line:** Incorporate **provincially in BC** (not federally), as a numbered company,
> and **put both founders on regular T4 payroll** for their R&D time. Cost is ~$350–$700
> one-time. This single ~$400 decision is the gate to the two biggest prizes — **SR&ED tax
> credits** (~60–65% of eligible R&D salary back, refundable) and **most BC/federal grants**,
> nearly all of which require an incorporated entity.
>
> *All figures CAD, verified against official sources June 2026 — re-confirm at filing.*

---

## Why this memo comes first

The research found that **the overwhelming majority of funding programs require incorporation**, and the one that pays the most for genuine software R&D — SR&ED — *also* requires it. A pre-incorporation founder is locked out of: SR&ED (federal + BC), NRC IRAP, every Innovate BC grant, every wage subsidy, and BDC. Incorporation is not "paperwork to do eventually" — it is the load-bearing first move of the entire funding plan.

The good news: it's cheap, it's DIY-able, and for a BC company the right choice is unambiguous.

---

## Federal vs BC — BC wins clearly for a Kelowna company

A **federal** corporation cannot operate in BC on its own — BC law requires every federal corp to **also register extra-provincially in BC**. So the real comparison isn't "$200 federal vs $350 BC"; it's the federal-plus-BC stack vs BC alone:

| | Federal + mandatory BC extra-provincial registration | **BC provincial only** ✅ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up-front government fees (numbered co.) | $200 + $350 = **$550** | **$350** |
| Recurring annual filing | $12 + $43.39 = **$55.39/yr** (two filings, two clocks) | **$43.39/yr** (one filing) |
| Registries to track | Two | One |
| Name protection | Nationwide (you don't need it yet) | BC-wide (fine for a local launch) |

**Federal buys you nationwide name protection you don't need yet, in exchange for a second registration and a second perpetual annual filing.** For a BC-based, BC-operating startup, BC-only is cheaper and simpler. If national branding matters later, protect it with a trademark — not a federal incorporation.

### CCPC / SR&ED status is identical either way — confirmed
The worry "does federal vs BC affect SR&ED?" is resolved: **CCPC status does not depend on incorporation jurisdiction.** The CRA test is (private) + (Canadian-resident) + (incorporated in Canada — *federal OR a province both count*) + (not controlled by non-residents/public corps). BC incorporation preserves the enhanced 35% refundable federal SR&ED credit and the refundable 10% BC credit fully. *The thing that can cost you CCPC status is taking foreign/public-corp control (e.g. a foreign-led VC round) — never your province of incorporation.*

*Sources: [ised-isde.canada.ca corp services & fees](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/corporations-canada/en/services-fees-and-processing-times); [BC Registries fee schedule](https://www.bcregistry.ca/WebHelp/fee_schedule.htm); [CRA Type of corporation](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/corporations/type-corporation.html); [BC SR&ED credit](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/income-taxes/corporate/credits/scientific-research-development).*

## How to incorporate — three routes, cheapest sensible one recommended

| Route | All-in cost (BC) | What you get | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **DIY numbered co.** via [Corporate Online](https://www.corporateonline.gov.bc.ca) | **~$350** (skip the $30 name request by using the incorporation number) | Legal shell only — *you* hand-build share issuance, resolutions, minute book | The floor; fine if you're comfortable doing the org paperwork correctly |
| **Ownr (BC)** ✅ recommended | **$699, ~$399 net** after the $300 RBC business-account rebate | Auto-generates articles, by-laws, organizational resolutions, **share issuance**, digital minute book | Best value for two brothers splitting equity — gets share issuance right, which is the #1 thing CRA cares about |
| **Lawyer** | ~$1,400–$2,200 + ~$1,500–$3,500 for a shareholders' agreement | Tailored structure + minute book + the agreement | Overkill for the shell; worth it *only* for the co-founder shareholders' agreement |

**Recommendation:** Incorporate via **Ownr BC (~$399 net)**, or DIY numbered (~$350) if you're confident with the share-issuance paperwork. Either way, then add a **simple two-founder shareholders' agreement** (vesting, departure/buy-sell, deadlock) — the one piece genuinely worth a lawyer when 50/50 brothers are involved, and exactly the legal/finance gap the partner brief already flags.

## ⚠️ The non-obvious part that makes or breaks SR&ED: pay yourselves T4 salary

This is the single most important operational consequence of incorporating, and it's the thing pre-revenue founders most often get wrong:

- **SR&ED labour credits are driven by T4 salary/wages.** Dividends are *not* salary and are categorically excluded. **Unpaid founder time creates nothing to claim.**
- A 2-founder team taking **dividends-only or no pay has almost nothing claimable** — even for textbook-eligible R&D.
- To capture SR&ED (~60–65% of eligible R&D salary back, refundable), the corporation **must run actual, regular T4 payroll for the founders' R&D hours** during the fiscal year.
- Both founders own >10%, so they're "specified employees": pay must be **regular salary, not year-end bonuses or profit distributions** (those are excluded for owners).

**Action:** once incorporated, set up payroll (CRA RP account) and pay each founder a regular T4 salary for the time spent on R&D. Discuss the salary-vs-dividend mix with an accountant *before* year-end — this decision determines whether SR&ED is worth tens of thousands or nothing. *(Bonus: a CRA business number + RP payroll account is also a prerequisite for Canada Summer Jobs and other wage subsidies.)*

*Source: [SR&ED Salary or Wages Policy](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/scientific-research-experimental-development-tax-incentive-program/salary-wages-policy.html); [Prescribed Proxy Amount Policy](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/scientific-research-experimental-development-tax-incentive-program/prescribed-proxy-amount-policy.html).*

## What incorporating unlocks (the payoff)

| Unlocked immediately | Unlocked once operating / with a hire |
| --- | --- |
| **SR&ED** record-keeping + future claim (federal 35% + BC 10%, refundable) — *the big one* | Innovate BC **Tech Co-op Grant** ($5K/student, $20K/yr) |

## Decision checklist

- [ ] Confirm BC incorporation (not federal) — cheaper + simpler for a Kelowna company.
- [ ] Choose route: **Ownr BC (~$399 net)** recommended, or DIY numbered (~$350).
- [ ] Numbered company now; add a trade name / brand later if desired (saves the $30 name request and avoids locking a name).
- [ ] Draft a **two-founder shareholders' agreement** (the lawyer-worthy piece).
- [ ] Register for a **CRA business number + RP payroll account**.
- [ ] Set up **T4 payroll for both founders' R&D time** (the SR&ED enabler) — confirm salary/dividend mix with an accountant before year-end.
- [ ] Pick a fiscal year-end with the accountant (affects when the first SR&ED claim lands).

*Open items to verify before paying: Ownr's live BC price + RBC rebate at signup; whether you want a named company from day one (brand) vs numbered + DBA later.*
