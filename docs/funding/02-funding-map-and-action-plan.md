## The one-screen summary

For a solo/duo pre-revenue founder, **most marquee grants are gated** and a few headline programs are confirmed dead-ends. The highest-leverage real plan is small and sharp:

1. **Incorporate in BC (~$400)** → unlocks SR&ED + nearly everything else.
2. **Run T4 founder payroll** → makes SR&ED actually worth claiming (~60–65% of R&D salary back).
3. **Engage Futurpreneur** (if a founder is ≤39) and/or **Community Futures Central Okanagan** → up to $75K + $150K in non-dilutive loans.
4. **Start the free NRC IRAP advisor relationship** → advice now, possible funding later.
5. **Prep the New Ventures BC Competition** entry for **Feb 2027** → $250K+ non-dilutive prize pool, no equity.
6. **Layer wage subsidies (SWPP / Tech Co-op / IRAP Youth)** once you're ready to hire.

That stack is realistic, non-dilutive, and mostly free to pursue. The big-ticket grants (Go-To-Market Microgrant, CanExport) come *after* you have traction/revenue/employees — they're milestones, not current applications.

### 🔴 DROP — confirmed non-fits (don't spend time here)

| Program | Why it does **not** fit Blue Seal |
| --- | --- |
| **BC Interactive Digital Media Tax Credit (IDMTC)** | A transactional trades marketplace **likely fails the "primarily to educate, inform or entertain" product test** (online-service/marketplace products are excluded). Even if it qualified, it needs **$100K+ in BC employee wages** (you have $0 payroll), and **you can't claim both IDMTC and BC SR&ED in the same year** anyway — SR&ED is the right path. |
| **Innovate BC Ignite** | Requires a **BC industry + BC academic consortium** in **natural resources / engineering / applied sciences**, funds *projects* via the academic institution, not companies. A general software startup with no academic partner is ineligible on multiple grounds. |
| **Mitacs Accelerate Entrepreneur** | The only Mitacs path that funds a *founder* — but requires the founder be a **current student / postdoc / grad within 2 years**, plus an academic supervisor and a Mitacs-approved incubator. Doesn't fit unless a co-founder is a recent student. (Listed here, not Tier 1, for that reason — revisit only if the profile fits.) |
| **Canada Digital Adoption Program (CDAP)** | **Closed.** Don't chase it or "successors" — none replace it for this use case. |
| **CSBFP (Canada Small Business Financing Program)** | Passes the revenue test (pre-revenue OK) but it's an **asset/equipment purchase loan** — a poor fit for a SaaS build with little to no qualifying physical purchases. |

## The highest-leverage stack (do these, in this order)

> One incorporation unlocks four non-dilutive sources. None of these take equity.

```
            ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
            │   STEP 0: Incorporate in BC (~$400)          │
            │   + run T4 founder payroll for R&D time      │
            └───────────────────┬─────────────────────────┘
                                │ unlocks
     ┌──────────────┬───────────┼───────────────┬──────────────────┐
     ▼              ▼           ▼                ▼                  ▼
 ┌─────────┐  ┌───────────┐  ┌──────────┐  ┌──────────────┐  ┌──────────────┐
 │ SR&ED   │  │Futurpreneur│ │Community │  │ NRC IRAP     │  │ New Ventures │

 │ of R&D  │  │ (if ≤39)   │ │ ≤$150K   │  │ → funding    │  │ $250K+ pool  │

 └─────────┘  └───────────┘  └──────────┘  └──────────────┘  └──────────────┘
   tax credit    loan           loan         advice→grant      competition
```

**The single highest-leverage move:** Incorporate + put founders on T4 payroll, then **run a disciplined SR&ED claim**. For genuine software/AI R&D, SR&ED is the largest, most repeatable, **non-dilutive** dollar amount available to you — and it recurs every year you do eligible R&D. Everything else is a complement to it.

## Apply in this order — the action plan

### Now (this month) — no incorporation needed to start
1. **Incorporate in BC** (`01`) — the gate. ~$400, ~a day of work via Ownr.
2. **Self-check the Futurpreneur age gate** — is either founder ≤39? If yes, `05` is live; start the business plan + 24-month cash flow.
3. **Call Community Futures Central Okanagan** (250-868-2132) to scope a startup loan (`06`).
4. **Start SR&ED record-keeping today** (`04`) — contemporaneous time tracking + the technical narrative. Even pre-incorporation, begin the discipline; the claimable clock starts at incorporation + payroll.

### Within ~30 days of incorporating
5. **Set up CRA payroll (RP account)** and put both founders on T4 salary for R&D time (`01`, `04`).
6. **Call NRC IRAP** (1-877-994-4727) to start the free ITA advisor relationship (`06`).
7. **If hiring a co-op student:** apply to **SWPP** (open for Summer 2026) and watch **Tech Co-op Grant** for reopen (`06`).

### Ongoing / scheduled
8. **New Ventures BC Competition** — applications open **~Feb 2027**. Use `03` (drafted entry) and submit Round 1 when it opens (~$200 entry fee).
9. **File the first SR&ED claim** after your first fiscal year-end (T2 + T661) — consider CRA's new **Pre-Claim Approval** (live Apr 1, 2026) for binding eligibility certainty first (`04`).

### Later milestones (don't apply early — you'll just get rejected)
10. **Go-To-Market Microgrant** once you have paid product-market fit.
11. **CanExport / ISI / BDC** once you have employees + revenue + operating history.

## Honest expectations

- **No magic grant writes you a cheque pre-incorporation, pre-revenue.** The real money for you is **SR&ED** (earned by doing + recording R&D and paying T4 salary) and **non-dilutive loans** (Futurpreneur, Community Futures) — plus a genuine shot at the **New Ventures BC** prize pool in 2027.
- **Incorporation pays for itself many times over** — a single modest SR&ED claim dwarfs the ~$400 cost.
- **Deadlines move and programs open/close** — the dated statuses here are June 2026 snapshots. Re-confirm each program's page before filing; the per-program files carry the official URLs.
