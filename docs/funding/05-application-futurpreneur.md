## ✍️ DRAFT — answers to the core questions (in the founders' voice)

> Grounded in the live product; edit to fit Futurpreneur's exact form fields.

**Describe your business.**
> Blue Seal is a progressive web app that verifies skilled tradespeople and runs the whole job from quote to payment. We hand-check each tradesperson's trade certification and government ID before their profile goes live, then give clients and pros one place to search, quote, chat, schedule, invoice, pay, and review each other — with AI built into each step. It's live in BC's Okanagan with roughly 100 verified tradespeople across 134 trades. We earn through a capped 5% client service fee and a $29/month "Blue Seal Pro" subscription for tradespeople.

**What problem do you solve, and for whom?**
> Homeowners can't tell a genuinely qualified tradesperson from someone who just claims a trade — almost no one checks the certification or ID. And skilled tradespeople, excellent at their craft, run their business on texts and spreadsheets while competing on ad spend instead of quality. Blue Seal serves both: verified trust for homeowners, and modern tools to win and manage work for tradespeople — timely as BC faces a shortage of roughly 52,600 tradespeople this decade.

**Why are you the right team?**
> Two brothers with complementary skills. James is a Red Seal tradesperson with 20+ years in the trades who lived this problem building his own home — he leads product, sales, and marketing (and is a professional photographer). Johnny is an award-winning creative director who builds and maintains the platform and owns the brand. Trade credibility plus technical and brand execution — and Futurpreneur's mentorship would directly strengthen our finance and operations side.

**How will you use the loan?**
> Primarily local demand marketing in the Okanagan (acquiring clients onto an already-stocked, verified supply side), plus incorporation/legal setup, a security and QA pass, accounting setup, and app-store presence. The product is already built and live, so this is a go-to-market loan, not a build loan — it funds turning verified supply into paying transactions, which generates the revenue to repay it. *(Mirror the partner-brief allocation; right-size to the loan amount.)*

**How will you repay it?**
> Revenue comes from the capped service fee on card payments and Blue Seal Pro subscriptions ($29/mo, ~$300 contribution per pro per year at near-zero acquisition cost). As verified pros convert to Pro and client transactions grow, recurring subscription revenue covers repayment. *(Back with the 24-month cash flow.)*
